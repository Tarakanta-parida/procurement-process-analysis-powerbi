# 📊 Procurement Process Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive, multi-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze procurement spending, supplier risks, product performance, and cost optimization opportunities.

This project demonstrates how data analytics can help procurement teams improve spending visibility, identify supply chain risks, and make informed purchasing decisions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Procurement Process Analysis Dashboard provides a consolidated view of procurement operations through three analytical dashboards and dedicated overview and conclusion pages.

The report focuses on:
- Procurement spending and product distribution.
- Supplier regions and Kraljic category analysis.
- High-risk products and single-source dependencies.
- Supply risk versus profit impact.
- Cost optimization and long lead-time products.
- Potential savings opportunities.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor total procurement expenditure.
- Identify products with high supply risk.
- Analyze supplier dependency and sourcing risks.
- Understand the relationship between supply risk and profit impact.
- Identify high-cost products and estimated savings opportunities.
- Support procurement planning through data-driven insights.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** – Measures, calculated columns, and KPI calculations.
- **Data Modeling** – Organizing procurement data for analysis.
- **Data Visualization** – KPI cards, bar charts, donut charts, scatter plots, treemaps, and tables.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

1. **Spending Visibility:** Identify products and supplier regions that account for a significant share of procurement spending.
2. **Supply Chain Risk:** Highlight products with higher supply risk and potential sourcing dependencies.
3. **Critical Products:** Prioritize products with both high supply risk and high profit impact.
4. **Cost Optimization:** Identify products that may require price reviews and supplier negotiations.
5. **Lead-Time Management:** Highlight products with longer lead times for proactive procurement planning.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Review purchasing prices for high-cost products.
- Explore alternative suppliers for single-source products.
- Prioritize risk mitigation for critical products.
- Review safety-stock requirements for long lead-time items.
- Monitor procurement spending and supplier performance regularly.

## 🚀 How to Use This Project

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Download and install Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Open the `.pbix` file using Power BI Desktop.
4. If required, update the dataset file path in Power Query.
5. Refresh the data to load the latest information.
6. Explore the report using interactive slicers and filters.

## 📌 Project Limitations

- This is a portfolio and learning project.
- Some procurement fields may be simulated for analytical practice.
- Potential savings depend on the assumed target prices or budget values.
- The findings should be validated against real procurement data before making actual business decisions.
